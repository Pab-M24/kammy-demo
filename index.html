How the money moves

  1. 01

    Fan picks a ticket and runs a soft check. Seconds, no impact on their credit score.

  2. 02

    The licensed partner underwrites and pays the organizer 100%, upfront, today.

    SOFOM in Mexico · NBFC in India

  3. 03

    The fan repays the lending partner over 3, 6 or 9 months. Kammy is not the creditor.

  4. 04

    Kammy earns 4% from the organizer and 5% from the lender. The fan pays Kammy $0.

Kammy never lends and never holds funds. No balance sheet, no credit risk, no float.
